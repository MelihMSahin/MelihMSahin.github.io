Firebrand · team project

CompanyLens MVP for Deloitte

A five-person team built and presented a company-search and client-onboarding research MVP. Melih's main role was the backend.

Situation

As part of Deloitte training delivered through Firebrand, a five-person team built a website MVP for company search and client-onboarding research.

Task

Build the MVP as a team and present it to Deloitte directors.

Contribution and decisions

Melih's main role was the backend. He contributed to deciding which requirements were Must-have or Should-have, proposed a star-style architecture with the backend as its central hub, and proposed separate API paths for distinct input types such as exact-match IDs and names.

Outcome

The team presented the MVP to Deloitte directors. Melih reports that the directors responded positively to the project and the team's design discussions and choices.
