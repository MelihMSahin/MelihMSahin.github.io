Deloitte training · Firebrand

CompanyLens MVP

28 September–2 October 2026

Situation

As part of Deloitte training delivered through Firebrand, a five-person team built a website MVP for company search and client-onboarding research. The public project repository describes CompanyLens as using an ASP.NET backend, Angular frontend, and SQLite database.

Task

The team built the MVP and presented it to Deloitte directors.

Contribution

Melih's main role was the backend. He contributed to requirements prioritization, deciding whether tasks were Must-have or Should-have, and proposed a star-style architecture with the backend as a central hub to avoid direct coupling among the front end, database, and API.

He also proposed separate API paths for distinct input types, such as exact-match IDs versus names, to improve separation of concerns and validation. He took a major part in leading technical discussions and systems design.

During the two weeks of online training before the project, the team learned Angular, C#, and Azure fundamentals.

Outcome

The team presented the MVP to Deloitte directors. Melih reports that the directors responded positively to the project and the team's design discussions and choices.

Evidence limits

This is a team MVP and presentation, not evidence of production use or adoption. No measured outcome is available. The director response is Melih's report. The testimonial below is qualitative corroboration; it is not a measured result or proof of seniority.
